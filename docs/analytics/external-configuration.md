# External setup — proposed

Five priorities. None has been applied yet.

1. **Identify traffic sources.** Tag links from ChatGPT, social posts and emails; check any available listing statistics.
2. **Verify tracking.** Confirm Plausible records the key funnel steps and Stripe reports payments correctly.
3. **Catch failures.** Add simple alerts for signup, connection, posting and payment problems.
4. **Keep comparisons clean.** Exclude test accounts and record the dates of launches and product changes.
5. **Set data retention.** Choose how long to keep analytics and check that the privacy wording covers it.
